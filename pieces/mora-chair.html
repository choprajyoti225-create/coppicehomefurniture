Mora Chair

Steam-bent crest rail, 3.1 kg, stacks three high.

£645

From, in soap finish
7–9 weeks at the bench

Light enough that a seven-year-old can carry it to the table and stiff enough that a grown adult can tip back in it, which they will. The crest rail is steam-bent over a former and left to season a fortnight before it is fitted.

Seats are woven Danish paper cord or a solid saddled panel, hollowed with a travisher to about 14 mm at the deepest point. The paper cord takes a maker roughly four hours per chair and can be rewoven in twenty years for the cost of the cord.

Keeping it

The repair is part of the design.

We keep the drawing for every Mora Chair we have shipped, filed under the owner’s surname. That means a repair can be quoted from a phone call: we already know the joint sizes, the finish, and which of us built it.

Wax, cord, webbing and touch-up oil are posted out free for the first ten years. After that they cost what they cost us. Bench repairs are charged at £58 an hour, and we will always tell you when a repair is not worth doing.
