Haldane Lounge Chair

A 14° back rake, arrived at by sitting in nineteen wrong ones.

£2,140

From, in soap finish
8–10 weeks at the bench

The chair took eleven prototypes and four of them are still in the workshop being sat on at lunch. The back rakes 14° and the lumbar swell sits 210 mm above the seat, which is where most adult spines want a hand placed.

Arms are steam-bent in one piece and let into the rear stile with a wedged through-tenon you can see from the side. It reads as a decorative detail. It is actually the reason the joint will outlast the upholstery.

Keeping it

The repair is part of the design.

We keep the drawing for every Haldane Lounge Chair we have shipped, filed under the owner’s surname. That means a repair can be quoted from a phone call: we already know the joint sizes, the finish, and which of us built it.

Wax, cord, webbing and touch-up oil are posted out free for the first ten years. After that they cost what they cost us. Bench repairs are charged at £58 an hour, and we will always tell you when a repair is not worth doing.
