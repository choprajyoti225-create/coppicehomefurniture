Quarry Shelving

A 320 mm grid you can extend in 2031 and still match the wood.

£1,940

From, in soap finish
8–10 weeks at the bench

Uprights and rails on a 320 mm module. Bays are 800 mm wide and take 42 kg of books each without visible deflection — we test to 60 kg and stop because the shelf stops being interesting after that.

We hold the same log run for the life of the line, so a bay added years later will match in colour and figure. Ships as a flat bundle; assembles with a mallet and the supplied oak wedges.

Keeping it

The repair is part of the design.

We keep the drawing for every Quarry Shelving we have shipped, filed under the owner’s surname. That means a repair can be quoted from a phone call: we already know the joint sizes, the finish, and which of us built it.

Wax, cord, webbing and touch-up oil are posted out free for the first ten years. After that they cost what they cost us. Bench repairs are charged at £58 an hour, and we will always tell you when a repair is not worth doing.
