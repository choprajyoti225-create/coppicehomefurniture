Linden Wardrobe

Frame-and-panel doors that stay flat in a room with one cold wall.

£4,720

From, in soap finish
14–18 weeks at the bench

Slab doors cup. Frame-and-panel doors do not, because the panel floats dry in the groove and moves independently of the frame holding it square. This is a four-hundred-year-old answer to a problem most wardrobes still have.

Inside: a solid ash hanging rail on turned ends, four adjustable shelves on the Quarry 320 mm module, and cedar strips let into the base — no bag, no crystals, just cedar.

Keeping it

The repair is part of the design.

We keep the drawing for every Linden Wardrobe we have shipped, filed under the owner’s surname. That means a repair can be quoted from a phone call: we already know the joint sizes, the finish, and which of us built it.

Wax, cord, webbing and touch-up oil are posted out free for the first ten years. After that they cost what they cost us. Bench repairs are charged at £58 an hour, and we will always tell you when a repair is not worth doing.
