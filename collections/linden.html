Linden

Quiet furniture for the hours nobody sees.

Notes on the collection

Parts, flat-packed

5

Pieces in the collection

3

A bed is the only piece of furniture that has to be silent. We spent most of a year on that problem and very little of it on how the headboard looked. The slat deck floats on cotton webbing rather than resting in a metal channel, which removes the two noises people complain about: the tick of timber on steel and the creak of a slat rolling in its seat.

Cases are frame-and-panel linden, a wood that stays pale for decades and takes a soap finish beautifully. Drawers run on wooden runners waxed with beeswax and a little tallow — heavier in the hand than a ball-bearing slide, and quieter by a wide margin. They will need re-waxing in about eight years. We send the wax.

The bed knocks down to five parts and reassembles with a hex key in under ten minutes, because the average flat in this country has a staircase that was designed to defeat furniture.
