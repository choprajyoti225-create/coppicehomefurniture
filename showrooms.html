What a visit is like

An hour, a kettle, and no clipboard.

Sit on everything

Every seat in the room is a production piece, not a display frame. Take your shoes off if you like — the Haldane is meant for it.

Handle the finishes

Four finished samples of each species, plus a board that has been in the Edinburgh window since 2018 so you can see how the colour actually moves.

Bring the room with you

Measurements, a photograph of the wall, the rug you refuse to part with. We will chalk your table length onto the floor in the showroom.

Leave without buying

There is no commission on our floor. Most people visit twice before ordering, and we would rather that than a returned sofa.
