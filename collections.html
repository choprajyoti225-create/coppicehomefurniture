Catalogue

Four collections,
twelve pieces,
no seasons.

We have added one collection every two or three years and retired nothing. A piece stays in the catalogue for as long as we can get the timber and repair what we have already sold — which, so far, is all of it.

Haldane

2019 — present

Seating that earns the dent it leaves in a rug.

Low, deep, and unhurried. Frames in air-dried ash, webbing hand-strung, cushions filled to sit soft on the first day and softer in the ninth year.

Mora

2021 — present

A table is a room with a top on it.

Solid oak tops on a splayed trestle, joined with drawbored tenons and no hardware. Built to seat eight and to be moved by two.

Dining rooms — read the collection

Linden

2020 — present

Quiet furniture for the hours nobody sees.

Beds and cases in pale linden and ash, with soft-stop drawers, cotton-webbed slats, and not a single squeak we could not chase down.

Bedrooms — read the collection

Quarry

2023 — present

Storage with the structure left showing.

Desks, shelving, and sideboards built on an exposed post-and-rail grid. Add a bay in an afternoon; the system was drawn to be extended.
